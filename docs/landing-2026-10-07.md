# Cinema-led landing page — 2026-10-07

## Direction

Reference pages inspected: [Netflix](https://www.netflix.com/jp-en/), [U-NEXT](https://video.unext.jp/) and [Jellyfin](https://jellyfin.org/). Borrow the emphasis on entertainment imagery and a clear next action, and Jellyfin's use of real product screenshots. Do not copy their brands or imply that Virelo provides a streaming catalogue.

Palette: cinema black #090909, reading surface #151515, primary text #f5f5f5, muted text #b2b2b2, restrained violet focus #cfb5ff. Existing Inter Variable keeps the landing page and product cohesive; bold display typography and quiet body copy carry the hierarchy.

Layout: flat navigation over a full-bleed original cinematic still; left-aligned two-line headline and launch action; large actual Home/Player screenshots; unframed feature copy; browser/CLI entry tabs; factual FAQ; closing action and source links. One memorable cinematic opening, no repeated animated cards, gradients used only to keep text readable over footage. No blur-text, spotlight cards, scroll reveals, fake device frames or invented product UI.

## Assets and provenance

The built-in image-generation tool created three original, text-free sample scenes. Generated artwork is only cinematic/background or sample-library content, not an image of an app UI. Sample titles, durations and histories are synthetic and explicitly disclosed on the landing page; no private user media, filenames or browser profiles were accessed.

Bundled assets: `web/public/landing/`.

- coast-cinema.webp: hero and sample-film artwork.
- night-cinema.webp and orbit-cinema.webp: isolated sample-library artwork used in the captured screens.
- library-desktop.webp, library-mobile.webp, player-desktop.webp and player-mobile.webp: screenshots of the current app, captured by scripts/capture-landing-demo.mjs with fresh browser storage and generated test footage. Codec preparation is stubbed for the screenshot fixture, not represented as a transcoding test.

Images are bundled locally and WebP encoded. Original generated PNGs remain under Codex generated_images. Raw captured PNGs are archived outside the public build; the page references only WebP assets.
